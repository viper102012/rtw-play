    Section 03 · being built as a desktop game

    Workshop: build anything in 3D

    The Workshop is becoming its own Windows game, made in the Godot engine. You'll snap parts together like Meccano or K'Nex, wire up the electronics, add code, and press Play to watch your creation run with real physics. The arm, gear and RC car tools that used to live here are now lessons, under Learn → Mechanics.

    Parts kits

    • Frame: beams, plates, brackets and extrusion with a grid of attach points
    • Motion: axles, bearings, hinges, gears, belts and pulleys, linear rails, springs, wheels, propellers
    • Motors: DC, servo and stepper, with real torque and speed limits
    • Electronics: Arduino boards, drivers, batteries, power supplies and the wires between their pins

    Things you'll be able to build

    • An RC car with body, axles, gears, springs and a wired-up driver
    • A drone, helicopter, boat or plane, with flying and floating physics
    • A robot arm like yours, driven by its own sketch
    • A 3D printer or laser cutter: frame, steppers, rails, belts and a print head or laser, running real movement code

    Play mode

    • A control panel of sliders, buttons and joysticks wired to your creation's inputs
    • Arduino code attached to a board, running inside the game
