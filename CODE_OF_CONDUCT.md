# Code of conduct

Tinker's Bot Workshop is a game for learning and building, and players of all ages and skill levels are welcome here.

## Please do

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to building, electronics or GitHub.
- Give feedback on the game, not the person.
- Keep bug reports to one problem each, with the steps to make it happen.
- Share your builds, tips and questions in Discussions.

## Please don't

- Post insults, harassment, or anything hateful or sexual.
- Post other people's personal information.
- Spam, advertise, or post links to copies of the game.
- Upload art, sounds or code that isn't yours.

## What happens if someone breaks these rules

The owner can edit or remove posts, lock threads, and block people from the repository. If something here bothers you, open a [Discussion](https://github.com/viper102012/rtw-play/discussions) in the *General* category or contact the owner on GitHub.
